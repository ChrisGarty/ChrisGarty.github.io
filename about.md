I’m Chris Garty, a youth soccer coach in Fargo, North Dakota. I coach players from U11 9v9 through U14 11v11 with Tri City United.

This site is where I work through what I’m learning: how to design better training, give players more ownership, coach the game without controlling it, and help young athletes grow into the demands of 11v11 soccer. My approach is informed by U.S. Soccer guidance and the Tri City United player-development curriculum, then shaped by what I see with players on the field each week.

The goal is practical usefulness. I want each article to offer an idea, a question, or an activity another coach can take to training and make their own.

## A personal coaching notebook

The views here are mine. This is an independent personal site, not an official publication of Tri City United, U.S. Soccer, or any other organization.
